Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective July 31, 2026

The short version: your diagrams and everything in them stay on your computer. Nothing you type, paste, or draw is ever sent to a server. The only thing this site tracks is anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics, like how many people visited and how many diagrams were created.

Your data stays local

Sankey Open Studio has no server, no database, and no accounts. Every diagram you create, including all names, amounts, colors, and settings, is stored only in your own browser's local storage on your own device. We cannot see your diagrams, and neither can anyone else. When you download an image or a JSON backup, the file is created on your computer by your browser.

Because storage is local, clearing your browser's site data will delete your saved diagrams. Use the JSON backup feature to keep copies you control.

What is tracked

This site uses Google Analytics to understand overall usage so the tool can be improved. That is the only tracking on the site. It records:

  • Standard page view information, such as pages visited, approximate region, browser type, and device type
  • Anonymous feature events: a diagram was created, data was pasted (a row count only), a diagram was exported (and in which format), and a backup was restored

What is never sent: the content of your diagrams. No node names, no labels, no amounts, no titles, no colors, and no file contents ever leave your browser as part of analytics or anything else.

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors. You can block these with a browser content blocker or by disabling cookies, and the app will keep working normally. Google's own handling of analytics data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. If you download this open source app and run it on your own computer or server, you can delete the analytics snippet entirely and nothing is tracked at all.
